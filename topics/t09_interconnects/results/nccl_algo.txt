A decode all-reduce at batch 1 is 7,168 B, which lands in the first regime: the
LL protocol on a SINGLE channel out of the 24 this node has. The 268,435,456 B
entry is the topology gate's 256 MB smoke test, at the other extreme.

What this capture establishes, and what it does not:

  OBSERVED  the algorithm is RING at every size; the protocol steps LL -> LL128 ->
            SIMPLE as size grows; the channel count steps 1 -> 24 over the same
            range; a decode-sized message uses exactly one channel; NVLS is
            unavailable on every device.

  INFERRED  that a one-channel LL handshake is *why* alpha does not grow with
            world size. This capture is consistent with that and rules out the
            competing algorithmic explanation, but it does not demonstrate the
            causal link. The measurement that tests the mechanism directly is the
            launch amortisation sweep (launch.py): the cost survives having every
            host-side launch hidden behind the previous call, so it is paid on the
            device, not in the dispatch path.

NVLS is unavailable on this node, so the multicast path that would collapse the
ring entirely was never an option here. On a node where it is available the
small-message numbers could differ, and this topic has not tested that.
